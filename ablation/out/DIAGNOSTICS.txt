(3) COLUMN-WISE vs ROW-WISE EXTRACTION FAILURE  [first-class result]
==============================================================================
A gold is SCATTERED in Arm B when its characters are largely present in a
single Arm B chunk (block-sum coverage >= 0.6) but cannot be
found as one run (contiguous coverage < 0.35). That is the
signature of PyMuPDF reading a row-wise table down its columns.

  slice          n  scattered    rate  median gap   p90 gap
  TABLE-GOLD    40          2   0.050       0.000     0.339
  PROSE-GOLD    40          0   0.000       0.000     0.000

  The binary criterion above is deliberately strict and, on its own,
  UNDERSTATES the effect. The graded distribution is the better view:

  scatter gap >     TABLE-GOLD    PROSE-GOLD
  0.05                17 / 40         1 / 40  
  0.10                11 / 40         1 / 40  
  0.20                10 / 40         1 / 40  
  0.30                 9 / 40         1 / 40  
  0.40                 4 / 40         1 / 40  

  Prose is the control, and it is clean: 1/40 at every threshold against
  10/40 for tables at gap > 0.20. The signal tracks table structure, not
  some general property of long chunks.

  WHY TABLE GOLDS FAIL IN ARM B (n=40, mutually exclusive):
    too long for any Arm B chunk :  12
    fits, but cells scattered    :   2
    fits, contiguous but < 0.60  :  13
    reaches 0.60 in Arm B        :  13

  Read this carefully before describing the mechanism. Cell scattering is
  REAL but is NOT the dominant failure: gold length (12) and plain low
  contiguous coverage (13) each account for more than scattering (2) under
  the strict definition. An earlier single-example reading of Q026
  over-generalised scattering as the main mechanism; this table supersedes
  it.
