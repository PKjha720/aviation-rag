MINIMUM DETECTABLE EFFECT - paired binary Recall, McNemar exact test
==========================================================================
alpha = 0.05 two-sided, power = 0.8. Not a measurement of this data:
the discordant rate is unknown until retrieval runs. Read the row that
matches the observed rate.

  n    = number of paired queries
  disc = fraction of queries where exactly one arm succeeds
  d    = number of discordant pairs the test actually uses
  MDE  = smallest difference in Recall (Arm A minus Arm B, in points of
         proportion) detectable at 80% power
  b*   = smallest number of Arm-A-only wins out of d that rejects H0

How to read it: at n=80 with a 20% discordant rate there are 16 usable
pairs, and the arms must disagree lopsidedly enough that the difference in
Recall is at least the MDE shown before an 80%-power test would call it.
Differences below the MDE are not detectable, whatever their sign.

Discordant pairs and the test statistic must be REPORTED alongside any
delta, because a delta with few discordant pairs is a delta the test
cannot see.
